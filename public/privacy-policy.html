Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to PixelCraft AI. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict operational standards regarding your personal information. Because our platform is fundamentally built on decentralized, client-side processing, our policy is incredibly simple: We do not collect, store, or sell your files.

2. Information Processing

PixelCraft AI operates via a transparent hybrid architecture. While many of our core tools run entirely within your browser (client-side), certain advanced features require server processing. For an itemized, tool-by-tool data processing breakdown, please consult our dedicated Privacy & Data Practices Inventory.

  • Fully Local Tools: Tools like Image Encryption, Steganography, Format Converter, and Metadata Removal process everything directly on your device. Your files never leave your browser sandbox.
  • Server-Processed Tools: Tools such as AI Dubbing, Forensic Sanitizer, and PDF Link Generator require secure server processing. For these tools, your files are transmitted to our servers and/or trusted third-party AI providers (Groq and Google Cloud) under ephemeral processing rules.
  • Your Output Data: Any generated media is rendered locally or securely downloaded to your device.

3. Log Files & Basic Analytics

In our commitment to privacy, PixelCraft AI limits telemetry. We collect basic usage metrics (such as tools used and session counts) linked to your account to improve our platform and enforce rate limits. We use Google Analytics for aggregated traffic analysis, which you can opt out of via our cookie consent banner.

4. Data Retention & Lifecycle Policy

We maintain an evidence-based, purpose-limited data retention policy across all platform services:

  • Temporary Shared Files: Uploaded PDF documents processed via our PDF-to-Link sharing tool are stored temporarily on isolated server storage and are automatically, permanently deleted after 24 hours. We do not inspect, index, or use user-uploaded documents for AI model training.
  • Authentication Sessions: Active user login sessions are maintained for a rolling period of 30 days from your last activity. Logging out immediately destroys the server session and invalidates the session cookie.
  • Security & Abuse Investigation Logs: In alignment with Indian cybersecurity directives (CERT-In Direction 2(iv) under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act), system security logs, honeypot activations, and abusive IP request records are maintained securely for a rolling window of 180 days for forensic investigation and attack mitigation. Records subject to a formal legal preservation hold are retained only for the duration of the justified inquiry.
  • Account Data: Registered user profiles are retained while your account remains active. You may request permanent account and personal data erasure at any time.
  • Client-Side Cryptography: For steganography, digital fingerprint wiping, and local privacy tools, all encryption keys, passwords, and media bytes remain entirely inside your browser memory and are never transmitted to our servers.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

PixelCraft AI's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers (such as Google) for more detailed information.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, PixelCraft AI uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

6. Third-Party Services & APIs

In order to provide advanced AI capabilities, PixelCraft AI integrates with select third-party services. When you use specific premium tools, necessary data (such as text or audio) may be securely transmitted to these providers:

  • Google TTS: Used for text-to-speech rendering in our AI Dubbing tools.
  • Groq & Hugging Face: Utilized for advanced natural language processing and AI inference.

We do not share any personally identifiable information (PII) with these services. We strictly limit the data shared to only what is necessary to perform the requested AI operation.

7. GDPR Privacy Rights (For EEA Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Our legal basis for processing your information relies on your consent and our legitimate interest in providing our services.

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions. Since we do not retain files beyond 24 hours, this primarily applies to your account credentials.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Data Portability: You may request restrictions on how we process your data or request a transfer of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
