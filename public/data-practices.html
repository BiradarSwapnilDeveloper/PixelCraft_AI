Client-Side Browser Sandboxing

Tools tagged as Local Browser execute exclusively inside your device's browser sandbox using standard Web APIs (HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, crypto.getRandomValues, and piexif.js).

  • Original media never leaves your computer or phone.
  • Steganographic secret messages and passwords are encrypted and processed locally.
  • No tracking tokens or media bytes are transmitted over the network for local tools.
Automated Storage Cleanup & Lifecycles

Server-assisted tools enforce strict, automated data lifecycles to prevent permanent data accumulation:

  • PDF-to-Link: Uploaded files are assigned cryptographic UUIDs and stored on isolated disk storage for up to 24 hours. An automated hourly cron job checks file creation timestamps and purges expired files.
  • AI Dubbing: Audio and video chunks are written to temporary system storage (os.tmpdir()) and deleted unlinkSync immediately after processing.
  • Forensic Sanitizer: Operates entirely in memory buffers (multer.memoryStorage()); nothing is written to disk.
Tool-Specific Cryptographic & Privacy Architecture Deep-Dive

Detailed technical audit of the internal data flows, cryptographic mechanisms, execution environments, and operational boundaries for PixelCraft AI's flagship privacy and anti-forensics tools.

🛡️ Advanced Decoy Steganography

  • 100% Client-Side Isolation: Executes exclusively in browser memory. Carrier images, secret text messages, decoy text, and encryption passwords never traverse the network (0 bytes transmitted to PixelCraft AI servers).
  • Authenticated Encryption (Encrypt-then-MAC): Plaintext payloads are encrypted with AES-256-CBC, and an HMAC-SHA256 authentication tag is computed over the ciphertext and IV. The tag is cryptographically verified before decryption, preventing padding oracle and bit-flipping attacks.
  • Scale-Invariant Luminance Macro-Grid: In Social Safe Mode, bits are modulated across an 80×80 macro-grid using normalized relative coordinates and 2D prime stride dispersion (stride S=521) with 3-pixel feathered center transitions, eliminating visible grid lines.
  • Capacity Tiers: Features 3 adaptive redundancy tiers: Ultra-Resilience (R=5, 5x majority voting for up to 30 chars), Balanced (R=3, 3x voting for 31–75 chars), and Extended (R=1 for 76–200 chars). For unbounded text or documents, Ultra-HD Lossless Mode uses spatial LSB bitplanes.
  • Plausible Deniability Limitations: The dual Real and Decoy channel design provides coercion mitigation by yielding different plaintexts based on the supplied password. It does not provide absolute mathematical immunity against sophisticated statistical steganalysis (e.g., chi-square or sample pair analysis).
  • External Transmission: Social media networks (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram) recompress images to lossy JPEG (Q~65). While Social Safe Mode is engineered to survive this re-encoding, heavy cropping, geometric rotation, or aggressive compression (Q<50) may render embedded data unrecoverable. Users must always retain unencrypted original backups.

🔬 Forensic Sanitizer & Cryptographic Scanner

  • Server-Assisted Volatile Execution: Uploaded images are received via POST /api/tools/forensic-sanitize and processed purely in volatile RAM buffers (multer.memoryStorage(), 50MB max limit). Buffers are never written to disk and are garbage-collected upon HTTP response completion.
  • Threat & Metadata Inspection: The engine audits for hidden trailing binary data beyond the standard JPEG (0xFFD9) or PNG (0x49454E44) EOF markers and parses EXIF metadata for camera serial numbers, device models, and GPS coordinates.
  • Metadata Deep-Scrubbing: Images are re-encoded through the high-performance Libvips engine (sharp) to strip all EXIF, XMP, IPTC, and proprietary vendor tags while preserving image dimensions and aspect ratios.
  • Dual-Anchor Cryptographic Sealing: Generates a server-signed HMAC-SHA256 seal. Anchor 1 embeds the seal hash in EXIF ImageDescription. Anchor 2 embeds a resilient watermark directly into the pixel luminance domain via scale-invariant macro-grid modulation.
  • Seal Verification Scope & Boundaries: POST /api/tools/verify-seal checks for valid seal recovery from metadata or pixel domains. Disclosed Limitation: Successfully recovering a seal confirms that an authentic PixelCraft seal was recovered; it does not constitute a legally binding guarantee that unmonitored visual elements were not altered after export.
  • Quality Metrics: Incorporates localized delta modulation maintaining PSNR ≥ 30.0 dB (benchmark: 33.51 dB, max pixel diff ≤ 13). Quality scores are technical measurements and do not guarantee identical human visual perception.

Account Data, Sessions, and Complete Deletion

PixelCraft AI is designed so that almost all tools can be used completely anonymously without creating an account. If you choose to log in with Google OAuth:

What We Store Upon Login

We store only your Google ID, primary email address, full name, profile picture URL, and a tool usage count in our MongoDB database. We never receive or store your Google account password.

Session Cookies & Duration

Authentication utilizes an HTTP-only, secure session cookie (connect.sid) with a rolling expiration window of 30 days. Logging out immediately destroys the server session and invalidates the cookie.

Account & Data Erasure

You can delete your account and associated database profile at any time through our verified POST /api/user/delete endpoint. Your profile record is permanently removed from the database immediately.

Important Technical Limitations & External Provider Disclosures

In compliance with our commitment to transparency, we do not make unsupported absolute claims such as "100% untraceable" or "zero-risk". Please be aware of the following technical boundaries:

Steganography, Plausible Deniability & Social Platform Delivery Advanced Decoy provides dual-password plausible deniability to mitigate coercion. However, steganography is not an absolute mathematical shield against specialized forensic steganalysis tools. When sharing embedded images via third-party messaging services (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram) or email gateways, platforms independently recompress, resize, or strip file data under their own policies. While Social Safe Mode is engineered for resilience against standard lossy JPEG re-encoding, transmission and extraction are not guaranteed under all arbitrary platform transformations. Always maintain unencrypted original backups.
Forensic Sanitization & Cryptographic Seal Verification Scope Forensic Sanitizer strips standard metadata tags and embeds a dual-anchor HMAC-SHA256 seal. Successful verification of this seal confirms that a valid PixelCraft seal was recovered from the metadata or pixel domain. Recovering a seal does not constitute a government-certified digital signature, legal non-repudiation attestation, or proof that unmonitored visual elements of the image have never been modified. Furthermore, metadata removal cannot eliminate camera sensor photo-response non-uniformity (PRNU) noise embedded in the optical pixels themselves.

While PixelCraft AI does not retain this media on its own servers, retention and processing on third-party provider infrastructure is governed by their respective privacy terms and beyond our physical control.
Public PDF Links & Search Indexing Files uploaded via PDF-to-Link receive an unguessable UUID. However, anyone who possesses this URL during the 24-hour lifetime can download the file. While we instruct search engines not to index uploaded PDF directories, users should exercise caution and avoid sharing confidential personal identifiers via public links.
Indian Cybersecurity Directions (CERT-In 180-Day Retention) PixelCraft AI operates in alignment with the Indian Information Technology Act (CERT-In Direction 2(iv)). System security logs, malicious traffic signatures, and honeytoken trap triggers are retained in tamper-resistant security logs for up to 180 days to investigate cyber attacks and unauthorized intrusions.
