3. Steganography, Social Media & Delivery Disclaimer

Steganography functions by embedding encrypted data within subtle visual noise variations of an image carrier. Users must be fully aware of the following technical boundaries:

  • No Guarantee of Undetectability: Steganography reduces casual detectability but does not guarantee mathematical invisibility against specialized forensic steganalysis tools (e.g., chi-square tests, sample-pair analysis, or machine-learning neural steganalysers).
  • Plausible Deniability Limitations: The separation of Real and Decoy channels via dual passwords is a software-level coercion mitigation feature. It is not an absolute legal defense or guaranteed protection against court-ordered disclosure.
  • External Transmission Limitations: Third-party platforms (including WhatsApp, Telegram, social media networks, and email clients) routinely recompress, resize, and re-encode images. While PixelCraft AI's Social Safe Mode is engineered for lossy JPEG resilience, recovery is not guaranteed across all third-party platforms or following aggressive geometric cropping, rotation, or heavy compression (Q<50).
  • No Data Recovery: Because encryption keys and plaintext messages are processed purely in local browser memory and never stored, PixelCraft AI cannot recover lost passwords or reconstruct corrupted files. Users must maintain unencrypted original backups.

4. Forensic Sanitization & Cryptographic Seal Disclaimer

Forensic Sanitizer provides deep EXIF/GPS metadata stripping and dual-anchor HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic sealing. Users acknowledge and accept the following boundaries:

  • Seal Verification Scope: Successful verification confirms that an authentic PixelCraft HMAC seal was recovered from metadata or the pixel domain. It does not certify that the image has never been modified outside the monitored luminance carrier, nor does it serve as a government-recognized digital signature or legal proof of chain-of-custody.
  • Metadata Removal Scope: Metadata sanitization removes standard EXIF, XMP, IPTC, and trailing EOF chunks. It cannot eliminate physical camera sensor imperfections (PRNU noise) or watermarks embedded within the visual pixel content itself.
  • Image Quality Metrics: Quality scores (such as PSNR ≥ 30.0 dB) are mathematical measurements of pixel deviation. They do not constitute a subjective aesthetic guarantee or promise of identical visual perception across all display hardware.
