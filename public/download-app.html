What is the PixelCraft AI Progressive Web App?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a modern software application built using open web technologies (HTML5, WebAssembly, CSS, and modern JavaScript) that delivers an experience identical to a native mobile or desktop application. When you install PixelCraft AI as a PWA, it runs in its own dedicated, standalone application window without browser navigation bars or clutter.

Unlike bulky store applications that require massive downloads, invasive permissions, and frequent multi-gigabyte updates, our PWA is exceptionally lightweight (under 5MB). Core assets and client-side processing engines are cached locally by a background Service Worker, enabling instant launches and seamless offline processing for all in-memory utilities.

How to Update the Progressive Web App

One of the greatest benefits of Progressive Web Apps is automated lifecycle management. You will never be interrupted by forced multi-gigabyte updates or app store approval delays:

  • Whenever you launch PixelCraft AI with an active internet connection, the background Service Worker checks for updated scripts and WebAssembly bundles.
  • New updates are downloaded quietly in the background without interrupting your current workflow.
  • When you close and reopen the app or refresh the window, the newly activated version immediately takes effect.

How to Uninstall the PixelCraft AI App

If you ever wish to remove the installed Progressive Web App, you can do so cleanly in seconds with zero lingering registry entries or leftover system files:

  • Android: Long-press the PixelCraft AI app icon on your home screen or app drawer → drag to "Uninstall" or tap the info icon → "Uninstall".
  • iOS: Long-press the app icon on your home screen → tap "Delete Bookmark" or "Remove App" → confirm deletion.
  • Windows (Chrome/Edge): Open the PixelCraft AI app window → click the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top title bar → select "Uninstall PixelCraft AI" → click "Remove". Alternatively, uninstall via Windows Settings → Apps → Installed Apps.
  • macOS: In Safari Web Apps, right-click the icon in your Dock → Options → Remove from Dock, then delete the shortcut from your ~/Applications directory. In Chrome, click the three-dot menu in the title bar → "Uninstall PixelCraft AI".

Is my privacy protected when running the installed app?
