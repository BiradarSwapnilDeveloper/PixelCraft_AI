Understanding the Phishing & Threat Scanner

Welcome to the PixelCraft AI Phishing & Threat Link Scanner. Malicious actors frequently employ deceptive links to conduct credential harvesting, spoof banking portals, and deliver malware payloads. This security utility provides deep heuristic analysis to help users evaluate whether a link is safe before navigating to it.

How the Scanner Inspects URLs

  1. Punycode & Homograph Detection: Identifies deceptive Unicode characters (e.g., Cyrillic 'а' replacing Latin 'a') designed to mimic legitimate domain names.
  2. Typosquatting & Subdomain Analysis: Analyzes deep subdomains and common brand misspellings often used in phishing lures.
  3. Shortener Unmasking: Safely queries short links via redirect tracing to reveal the final destination without executing browser code.
  4. Contextual AI Threat Scoring: Evaluates domain entropy and threat databases to compute an overall risk score from 0 (Safe) to 100 (Critical Threat).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the URL Phishing Scanner detect threats?

The scanner executes multi-layered heuristics against submitted links via /api/scan-link, evaluating punycode homographs, subdomain entropy, known typosquatting patterns, and unmasked redirect endpoints in volatile memory.

Is the Phishing Scanner free to use?

Yes, completely free with no usage fees or registration required.

How is my scan data handled?

The target URL is submitted to our backend analysis engine via /api/scan-link and evaluated ephemerally in volatile memory. We do not maintain a permanent database of your submitted links, nor do we correlate scanned URLs with your user identity or IP address.
