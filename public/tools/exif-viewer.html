Analyzing Hidden Image Metadata (EXIF)

Digital cameras and smartphones embed rich metadata into every photograph, known as EXIF data. Our EXIF Viewer allows photographers and OSINT researchers to instantly read shutter speeds, ISO, focal lengths, and hidden GPS coordinates encoded in their media. Understanding what data you broadcast is the first step in digital hygiene. We process the image locally so your data is never logged by our servers.
