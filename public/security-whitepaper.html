01 / THREAT LANDSCAPE

1. The Problem Space: Covert Transport Over Hostile Networks

Modern digital communication channels are aggressively hostile to covert data transport. Transmitting encrypted information across social messaging platforms (e.g., WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal) or email gateways faces three catastrophic failure modes:

VULNERABILITY 01
Lossy Transcoding Wipe
Messaging platforms re-encode user images to lossy JPEG ($Q \approx 65\text{--}75$) with $4:2:0$ chroma subsampling. Conventional LSB (Least Significant Bit) steganography is 100% wiped in transit.
VULNERABILITY 02
Padding Oracle Malleability
Unauthenticated AES-CBC ciphers allow attackers or network proxies to flip bits in transit, producing padding oracle leaks and silent ciphertext corruption without cryptographic rejection.
VULNERABILITY 03
Container Tag Stripping
Social media platforms deep-strip all EXIF, XMP, and IPTC metadata headers, permanently destroying classical digital signatures, provenance tags, and forensic timestamps.

03 / CLIENT-SIDE STEGANOGRAPHY

3. Tool 1: Advanced Decoy Steganography Architecture

Advanced Decoy operates under a Client-Side Local Execution architecture. All image rasterization, luminance calculations, cryptographic transformations, and bit interleaving take place strictly inside the browser via the HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript engine (window.crypto and CryptoJS). Zero carrier or payload bytes are transmitted to remote servers.

Perceptual Luminance Encoding Formulation Human visual biology is drastically less sensitive to subtle luminance variance than to chroma shifts. The engine converts raw RGB pixel channels to ITU-R BT.601 perceptual luminance $Y$:
Formula 1: ITU-R BT.601 Perceptual Luminance Conversion
$$Y = 0.299 \cdot R + 0.587 \cdot G + 0.114 \cdot B$$

3.1 Scale-Invariant Macro-Grid Partitioning

The carrier image of dimensions $W \times H$ is partitioned into an $80 \times 80$ normalized grid ($M = 6,400$ total macro-cells). For each cell at grid position $(r, c)$ where $r \in [0, 79]$ and $c \in [0, 79]$, physical image boundaries are computed:

Formula 2: Normalized Coordinate Mapping
$$x_0 = \left\lfloor \frac{c \cdot W}{80} \right\rfloor, \quad x_1 = \left\lfloor \frac{(c + 1) \cdot W}{80} \right\rfloor, \quad x_{\text{mid}} = \left\lfloor \frac{x_0 + x_1}{2} \right\rfloor$$ $$y_0 = \left\lfloor \frac{r \cdot H}{80} \right\rfloor, \quad y_1 = \left\lfloor \frac{(r + 1) \cdot H}{80} \right\rfloor$$

Each cell is divided into Sub-cell A (left half: $x \in [x_0, x_{\text{mid}}-1]$) and Sub-cell B (right half: $x \in [x_{\text{mid}}, x_1-1]$). Let $\bar{Y}_A$ and $\bar{Y}_B$ be the arithmetic mean luminance:

Formula 3: Differential Luminance Modulation (ΔY)
$$\Delta Y = \bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B$$ $$\text{Target } \Delta Y = \begin{cases} +\delta & \text{if bit } b = 0 \\ -\delta & \text{if bit } b = 1 \end{cases}$$

The adjustment factor $\alpha = (\text{Target } \Delta Y - \Delta Y) / 2$ is applied to all RGB channels. Because JPEG compression averages neighboring pixels through low-pass DCT filters, the difference between sub-halves ($\bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B$) survives lossy compression even when individual pixel values shift.

04 / ANTI-ARTIFACT MATHEMATICS

4. 2D Prime Stride ($S = 521$) Dispersion & Feathering

In early implementations, sequential embedding from cell $0$ to $N$ created unnatural checkerboard blocks and localized blur in the upper portion of the image. PixelCraft v3.0 solves this via coprime spatial dispersion and 3-pixel feathered transitions.

4.1 Coprime Permutation Mapping

The total 6,400 cells are partitioned into 3,200 even cells (Slot 0: Real Payload) and 3,200 odd cells (Slot 1: Decoy Payload). Using the prime stride $S = 521$, where $\gcd(521, 3200) = 1$, the engine establishes a uniform bijection across the entire image:

Formula 4: 2D Coprime Stride Dispersion Mapping
$$\text{CellSlotIndex}(k) = (k \cdot 521) \pmod{3200}$$ $$\text{CellIndex}(k, \text{slot}) = 2 \cdot \text{CellSlotIndex}(k) + \text{slot}$$

4.2 Center Feathering & Luminance Clamping

To prevent sharp vertical boundary lines between Sub-cell A and Sub-cell B, a graduated horizontal transition weighting is enforced:

Formula 5: 3-Pixel Feathering Transition Weight
$$h_{\text{weight}}(x) = \begin{cases} +1.0 & x < x_{\text{mid}} - 1 \\ +0.45 & x = x_{\text{mid}} - 1 \\ -0.45 & x = x_{\text{mid}} \\ -1.0 & x > x_{\text{mid}} \end{cases}, \quad y_{\text{feather}}(y) = \begin{cases} 0.7 & \text{if boundary pixel} \\ 1.0 & \text{interior} \end{cases}$$
Dynamic Quality Gate: Calibrated PSNR > 38 dB Luminance modulation is dynamically dampened in extreme brightness ($\bar{Y} > 245$) and deep darkness ($\bar{Y} < 10$) from $\delta = 13$ down to $\delta = 7$. As a result, the Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR) consistently exceeds $38\text{--}42\text{ dB}$, rendering alterations imperceptible to human visual inspection.

4.3 Cryptographic Encrypt-then-MAC Authentication

The engine rejects unauthenticated CBC mode. The cipher payload is framed with a 64-bit truncated HMAC-SHA256 authentication tag:

Formula 6: Key Separation & Authenticated Framing
$$K_{\text{enc}} = \text{SHA256}(\text{pwd}), \quad K_{\text{mac}} = \text{SHA256}(\text{pwd} \parallel \text{"::PixelCraft\_Auth\_MAC"})$$ $$\text{Payload} = \texttt{0x4554 ('ET')} \parallel IV_{(16\text{B})} \parallel \text{HMAC-SHA256}(IV \parallel C, K_{\text{mac}})_{[0..7]} \parallel C_{\text{AES-CBC}}$$
05 / SERVER-ASSISTED EPHEMERAL PIPELINE

5. Tool 2: Forensic Sanitizer & Ephemeral RAM Architecture

Forensic Sanitizer processes uploaded images via a dedicated backend in server.js. Unlike traditional cloud converters that write user files to storage buckets or temp folders, PixelCraft AI operates under a Zero-Disk Retention Policy.

STAGE 01
Volatile RAM Ingestion
Files stream directly into Node.js volatile memory buffers via multer.memoryStorage(). Max limit: 50MB. Zero bytes touch persistent disks.
STAGE 02
Hex EOF Malware Scan
Binary analysis scans byte sequences beyond formal image EOF markers (JPEG FF D9, PNG IEND) to detect appended malware payloads.
STAGE 03
Libvips Deep-Strip
Sharp C++ pipeline auto-rotates and re-encodes into clean PNG, permanently stripping EXIF, GPS, camera serial numbers, and private metadata chunks.
STAGE 04
Immediate Memory Purge
Once the response payload streams to the user, V8 buffers are dereferenced and purged via garbage collection.
06 / PROVENANCE VERIFICATION

6. The Dual-Anchor Cryptographic Seal Engine

When an image is sanitized, proving that it has not been modified post-sanitization is critical. Conventional tools write an EXIF tag, which social networks immediately wipe. PixelCraft AI invents the Dual-Anchor Seal Engine:

Verification Anchor Physical Location Survives Social Transcoding? Extraction Mechanism
Anchor 1 (Container) EXIF IFD0:ImageDescription Tag No (Wiped by messaging apps) Fast header read (ExifReader.load())
Anchor 2 (Deep Pixel) $40 \times 40$ Luminance Parity Macro-Grid Yes (>= 99% Recovery) Deep Pixel Luminance Engine (0x534C sync magic)
Formula 7: Cryptographic Seal Digest & Deep-Pixel Embedding
$$\text{SealHash} = \text{HMAC-SHA256}(\text{ScrubbedBuffer}, \text{SEAL\_SECRET})$$ $$\text{Pixel Stream} = \texttt{0x534C ('SL')} \parallel \text{SealHash}_{(256\text{b})} = 272 \text{ Total Bits}$$

The 272 bits are embedded across a $40 \times 40$ macro-grid ($1,600$ cells) with differential luminance $\Delta Y = \pm 14$. When a user uploads an image to /api/tools/verify-seal:

  1. Primary Check: The server inspects the EXIF header. If present, the seal is verified immediately.
  2. Deep-Pixel Fallback: If EXIF is stripped (e.g. WhatsApp download), the server parses the $40 \times 40$ macro-grid, recovers the 256-bit hash, and authenticates the image with the report: "EXIF stripped by messaging app; Authenticated Cryptographic Seal recovered from Deep-Pixel Luminance Grid!"
07 / SECURITY AUDIT & BOUNDARIES

7. Threat Modeling & Operational Boundaries

In accordance with transparent disclosure standards, PixelCraft AI explicitly documents the realistic boundaries of its privacy defenses:

Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) Boundary Forensic Sanitizer strips all metadata tags and binary payloads. However, metadata removal cannot eliminate PRNU sensor noise—the microscopic silicon imperfections embedded in optical pixels by physical camera sensors. Users requiring nation-state OPSEC must apply Gaussian re-rasterization or synthetic noise layering.
Plausible Deniability Scope Advanced Decoy provides dual password slots (Real and Decoy). Under coercion, revealing the Decoy passphrase decrypts the innocent decoy payload while the Real slot remains indistinguishable from natural image noise. However, specialized statistical steganalysis (RS analysis) across multiple carriers could indicate non-random luminance variance. Steganography is an obfuscation aid, not a substitute for legal immunity.
