01 / THREAT LANDSCAPE

1. The Problem Space: Covert Transport & Media Surveillance Over Hostile Networks

Modern digital communication channels and cloud platforms are aggressively hostile to user privacy and covert data transport. Transmitting digital media across social gateways (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal) or centralized storage silos exposes users to four critical vulnerabilities:

VULNERABILITY 01
Lossy Transcoding Wipe
Social messaging gateways aggressively recompress media to lossy JPEG ($Q pprox 65 ext{--}75$) with $4:2:0$ chroma subsampling. Conventional LSB (Least Significant Bit) steganography is 100% wiped in transit.
VULNERABILITY 02
Sensor PRNU Tracking
Every physical camera sensor embeds microscopic silicon photo-diode non-uniformities (PRNU) into image pixels. Law enforcement and surveillance crawlers match photos across pseudonymous accounts via FFT cross-correlation.
VULNERABILITY 03
Cloud File Scraping
Traditional online format converters and PDF hosts retain user files on remote AWS S3 / disk buckets for days, exposing sensitive personal documents to data breaches, subpoenas, and unauthorized AI training scraping.
VULNERABILITY 04
SSRF & Phishing Cloaking
Phishing actors exploit Unicode homographs (Punycode confusables), multi-hop redirect chains, and shorteners, while malicious payloads attempt Server-Side Request Forgery against cloud metadata endpoints (169.254.169.254).

Tool 01: Advanced Decoy Steganography

100% Client-Side RAM Steganography & Cryptography
Launch Live Tool

Advanced Decoy executes Scale-Invariant Differential Luminance Macro-Grid Steganography entirely within the user's browser runtime. It provides dual-key plausible deniability (Real vs. Decoy slot), immune to lossy social media transcoding (WhatsApp/Telegram JPEG compression) and authenticated via Encrypt-then-MAC (AES-256-CBC + HMAC-SHA256).

A. Dataflow & Architectural Pipeline Map

STAGE 01
Dual-Key Derivation
Derives independent encryption ($K_{\text{enc}}$) and authentication ($K_{\text{mac}}$) keys from passphrase via SHA-256 with protocol salt separation.
STAGE 02
80x80 Macro-Grid Partition
Carrier image ($W \times H$) is mapped into 6,400 macro-cells, split into 3,200 even cells (Real) and 3,200 odd cells (Decoy).
STAGE 03
Coprime Stride (S=521) Dispersion
Permutes cell traversal via $\gcd(521, 3200)=1$ prime stride, preventing localized blurring or visual checkerboard artifacts.
STAGE 04
Differential Sub-Cell Modulation
Modulates arithmetic mean luminance between sub-cell halves ($\Delta Y = \bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B = \pm\delta$), surviving lossy DCT quantization.
STAGE 05
3-Pixel Feathering & PSNR Gate
Enforces graduated center transition weights ($h_{\text{weight}} = \pm 0.45$) and clamps $\delta$ in extremes, maintaining PSNR > 38 dB.

$$\Delta Y = \bar{Y}_A - \bar{Y}_B, \quad \text{Target } \Delta Y = \begin{cases} +\delta & \text{if bit } b = 0 \\ -\delta & \text{if bit } b = 1 \end{cases}$$

$$\text{CellSlot}(k) = (k \cdot 521) \pmod{3200}, \quad \text{CellIndex}(k, \text{slot}) = 2 \cdot \text{CellSlot}(k) + \text{slot}$$

Tool 04: Digital Fingerprint Wiper & PRNU Defense

100% Client-Side In-Memory Execution Forensics & Anonymity
Launch Live Tool

Digital Fingerprint Wiper neutralizes physical hardware camera sensor fingerprints. Every physical camera sensor possesses microscopic silicon photo-diode non-uniformities known as Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) noise. Forensic law enforcement tools extract PRNU patterns via Fast Fourier Transforms (FFT) to tie images to a suspect's phone. This tool injects stochastic micro-perturbations and purges MakerNote tags entirely in client RAM.

A. Dataflow & Architectural Pipeline Map

STAGE 01
MakerNote Hardware Strip
Decodes raw pixel buffers into Canvas, permanently discarding proprietary MakerNote tags containing camera body serials and lens IDs.
STAGE 02
High-Frequency Residual Extraction
Applies spatial discrete convolution to isolate microscopic high-frequency sensor noise residuals from underlying image textures.
STAGE 03
Stochastic Micro-Dithering
Injects imperceptible random noise ($\pm 1$ to $\pm 2$ discrete luminance steps out of 255) across randomized 64x64 sub-blocks.
STAGE 04
FFT Cross-Correlation Decoupling
Alters the spatial noise residual matrix, nullifying Circular Cross-Correlation and Peak-to-Correlation Energy (PCE).
STAGE 05
Verified Quality Gate
Verifies that the Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM) exceeds 0.994 before generating the pristine sanitized download blob.

B. Mathematical Formulations & Cryptographic Specifications

Formula 1: Physical Sensor Noise Model
$$I(x, y) = I_0(x, y) \cdot [1 + K(x, y)] + \Theta(x, y)$$ $$\text{where } K(x, y) \text{ represents physical PRNU silicon photo-diode non-uniformity.}$$
Formula 2: Circular Cross-Correlation via FFT
$$\rho(X, Y) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\left( \frac{\mathcal{F}(X) \cdot \mathcal{F}^*(Y)}{\|\mathcal{F}(X)\| \cdot \|\mathcal{F}(Y)\|} \right)$$
Formula 3: Stochastic Micro-Perturbation Mask
$$I_{\text{clean}}(x, y) = I(x, y) + \mathcal{U}(-1, 1) \cdot \lambda(x, y), \quad |I_{\text{clean}} - I| \le 2$$
Formula 4: Structural Similarity Index Measure (SSIM)
$$\text{SSIM}(I, I_{\text{clean}}) = \frac{(2\mu_I\mu_{\text{clean}} + C_1)(2\sigma_{I,\text{clean}} + C_2)}{(\mu_I^2 + \mu_{\text{clean}}^2 + C_1)(\sigma_I^2 + \sigma_{\text{clean}}^2 + C_2)} \ge 0.994$$
