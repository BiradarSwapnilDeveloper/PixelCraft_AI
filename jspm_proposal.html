1. Context & The Practical Challenge Faced by JSPM Students

During every academic cycle (Semester Examination forms, Hall Ticket registrations, College Admissions, MahaDBT scholarships, and placement drives), thousands of our fellow students encounter strict upload requirements:

  • Photographs must be strictly under 50 KB without facial distortion.
  • Signatures must be strictly under 20 KB.
  • Photos taken on modern smartphones (especially Apple iPhones in HEIC format) exceed 4 MB to 8 MB and are rejected by ERP upload forms.
  • Students must repeatedly merge multiple assignment pages, lab journals, and payment receipts into a single PDF.

Consequently, students often resort to unverified commercial websites or cyber cafes where sensitive identity proofs (Aadhaar cards, marksheets, and personal photos) are uploaded to unknown cloud servers.

3. Why Specific Tools are Perfect for JSPM University

While PixelCraft AI contains a wide security and forensics suite, the following four tools are exceptionally well-suited for direct integration into our JSPM University Student ERP, Admission, and Examination Portals:

  • Image Compressor (Target 20KB & 50KB Presets): Students can drag their photo, select the 20KB or 50KB preset, and get a portal-ready file in seconds without quality degradation.
  • Universal Format Converter (Resolving iPhone HEIC Issues): Eliminates the common complaint where Apple iPhone users cannot upload HEIC photos to the JSPM ERP portal.
  • Images to PDF Converter: Enables students to merge multiple assignment snapshots, fee payment receipts, and lab journals into a unified, lightweight PDF.
  • Smart Photo Resizer: Automates exact 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm passport-size dimension formatting for student identity cards, library cards, and convocation records.

4. Key Institutional Benefits for JSPM University

  • Zero Institutional Liability (100% Client-Side Privacy): All processing executes inside the user's browser memory (RAM) via HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. No student photographs or documents are ever uploaded to, or stored on, external cloud servers.
  • 100% Free Forever: Zero subscription fees, zero licensing costs, and no forced user sign-ups or commercial advertisements for students.
  • Frictionless 2-Line Integration: The university web team can deploy this utility as a simple helper button or an embedded iframe widget directly next to the portal upload buttons.
  • Championing Student Innovation: Deploying this solution highlights JSPM University's commitment to supporting practical, student-developed software solutions.
